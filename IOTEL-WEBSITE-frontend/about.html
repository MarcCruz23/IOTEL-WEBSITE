How to Use IOTEL

Follow these steps to get the most out of the platform:

1

Browse the Catalog

Go to Catalog in the top navigation. Filter by category, sort by price or name, and search for specific products. Click any product to see full details, specs, and availability.

2

Add Items to Your Cart

Click Add to Cart on any product. Adjust quantities in the Cart view — accessible via the shopping cart icon in the header. You can remove items or clear the cart at any time.

3

Checkout & Pay

Proceed to Checkout from your cart. Choose a saved shipping address or add a new one. Select a payment method: GCash, Bank Transfer, or Cash on Delivery. Review your order summary and confirm.

4

Track Your Orders

Visit the Orders section to see all your past and active orders. Filter by status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered) and view itemized receipts for each order.

5

Book a Service

Go to Services to view available service types. Select the service you need, pick a date and time slot, and submit your booking. Our team will confirm and reach out if needed.

6

Contact Support

Use the Messages section for direct communication with our support staff. You can also use the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner for quick questions at any time.

7

Manage Your Addresses

Access My Addresses from the user menu in the top-right. Add, edit, or remove shipping addresses so checkout is faster on future orders.
